On the work

I believe a room should feel settled before it feels styled. My work begins with light, proportion and a small, honest palette of materials — travertine, oak, plaster, wool — and ends only when a space feels quiet enough to live in.

The pages that follow gather projects across two worlds I move between: private residences — bedrooms, living rooms, family homes — and the shared interiors of residential buildings, from playrooms and lounges to lobbies and conference rooms. Different briefs, one sensibility: warmth, restraint, and a deep respect for the people who will use the space.

— Aisha
